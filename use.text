# Goal
(Multi Domain)


**Digital Maintenance Layer for Biomedical Systems**

NeuroCore is extended as a transition-sensitive supervisory layer for biomedical devices operating in safety-critical environments such as operating rooms and intensive care units. By transforming device telemetry x(t) into a functional stability representation z(t)=[Δ(t),K(t),L(t)], the framework enables continuous monitoring of structural system behavior rather than isolated parameter values.

This approach allows early detection of pre-failure conditions, computational anomalies, and instability patterns that may precede device malfunction. 

Unlike traditional periodic inspection, NeuroCore provides a continuous, model-based assessment of system integrity, supporting proactive maintenance and reducing reliance on manual intervention.

## Avalanche Safety and ARVA Systems
    
**11.1 Context and Motivation**

Avalanche safety systems rely on rapid detection, localization, and interpretation of signals under highly unstable environmental conditions.

Standard systems such as ARVA (Avalanche Transceivers) operate through:

- electromagnetic signal emission and reception
- directional signal tracking
- amplitude-based localization

However, these systems primarily focus on:

- signal presence
- signal strength
- spatial direction

and do not explicitly model:

the structural stability of the signal over time

**11.2 NeuroCore Integration in Avalanche Systems**

NeuroCore introduces a complementary analytical layer:
x(t)→z(t)
where:
x(t): ARVA signal (amplitude, phase, noise fluctuations)
z(t): functional representation of signal stability

4. False Signal Filtering
